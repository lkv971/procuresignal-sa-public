Smarter signals. Better decisions. Less tender noise.

The first version is designed around three practical questions: what fits, who is buying, and what needs verification?

◎

Opportunity-fit scoring

Prioritize public-sector opportunities based on category, province, timing, briefing risk, source quality, and business-profile fit.

◌

Buyer intelligence

Understand active buyers, repeat demand, category patterns, and useful history even when a record is no longer a live opportunity.

✓

Source-quality flags

Spot cancelled, duplicate, or mixed-status tender records so users know what to verify before spending time on a bid.

How it works

A lightweight intelligence workflow for SMEs that do not have a full-time bid intelligence team.

Create a business profile

Tell us your sector, service areas, capabilities, and tender workflow needs.

Score and classify opportunities

Separate live opportunities, buyer-intelligence records, and not-actionable records with transparent reasons.

Act with better context

Use fit scores, buyer signals, and source-quality flags to decide what deserves attention.

🛡

Independent intelligence layer. Not an official tender portal.

ProcureSignal SA is independent and is not affiliated with any South African government entity or issuing buyer. Users must always verify official tender documents on the official issuing authority source before acting.
