Privacy Notice

This notice explains the information ProcureSignal collects, why it is used, and the choices available to account holders and website visitors.

Independent product

ProcureSignal is independent and not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or operated by any government entity, public buyer, procurement authority, or official tender portal.

Information we collect

We may collect information that you submit or create, including:

  • name, company name, and business email address;
  • business category, service areas, capabilities, and preferences;
  • account, onboarding, billing, and subscription status;
  • saved opportunities, relevance feedback, and source feedback;
  • messages and support requests you send to ProcureSignal.

How we use information

We use this information to:

  • provide and secure the ProcureSignal account and workspace;
  • match and prioritize opportunities using the business profile;
  • operate subscriptions and company-based access;
  • improve source quality, scoring explanations, and usability;
  • respond to requests and important service communications.

Public procurement information

ProcureSignal processes public procurement metadata and official links from configured sources. Source records may name public buyers and supplier businesses where that information is publicly published as procurement lifecycle evidence. ProcureSignal does not use restricted supplier accounts or private submission workflows as a public data source.

Service providers and international processing

ProcureSignal uses service providers for hosting, authentication, database services, payment processing, monitoring, and communications. These providers may process data in countries other than your own under their applicable terms and safeguards.

Optional AI procurement assistant

When enabled and you explicitly submit a question, a third-party AI provider may receive your question and limited opportunity metadata for bounded procurement decision-support. This feature does not send document bodies, billing data, business-profile data, full account history, or previous answers. ProcureSignal does not maintain persistent assistant chat history.

Avoid unnecessary personal, confidential, or sensitive information in your questions. Public procurement metadata may itself contain personal information. Provider data handling and retention are separate from ProcureSignal retention; we do not promise zero retention. International processing may apply depending on the verified provider and account configuration.

Operational usage counters and security/cost-control records may be retained without retaining assistant questions, answers, or prompts. Use the privacy contact below for questions or requests about this processing.

Payments

Payment details are handled by the configured payment provider. ProcureSignal stores subscription and provider-reference information needed to manage access, but does not store full card details.

Retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably needed to provide the service, meet legal or recordkeeping needs, resolve disputes, and protect the platform. We use access controls and operational safeguards, but no internet service can promise absolute security.

Your choices

You may ask to access, correct, or delete information associated with you, subject to legal and operational retention requirements. Contact privacy@procuresignal.io.

Contact

Privacy questions: privacy@procuresignal.io. General questions: hello@procuresignal.io.
