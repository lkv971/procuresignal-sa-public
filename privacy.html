Privacy Notice

ProcureSignal is an independent procurement intelligence platform for procurement teams and growing businesses. This privacy notice explains how we collect and use information submitted through the public landing page, product workspace, account setup, and related communications.

ProcureSignal is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or operated by any government entity, issuing authority, issuing buyer, or official tender portal.

Information we collect

We may collect information that you choose to submit, including:

  • name;
  • company name;
  • email address;
  • website or LinkedIn page;
  • business category, service areas, capabilities, and tender workflow details;
  • feedback about tender discovery, buyer intelligence, pricing expectations, and product interest;
  • permission to contact you for follow-up or product access.

How we use the information

We use submitted information to:

  • understand whether ProcureSignal solves real procurement workflow problems;
  • prioritize product features and data sources;
  • respond to product access and account requests;
  • contact respondents who gave permission for follow-up;
  • improve the website, product workspace, scoring explanations, and feedback loop.

Product workspace use

When you use the ProcureSignal product workspace, we may collect basic product signals such as profile completion, saved opportunities, dismissed opportunities, relevance feedback, source-quality feedback, and comments you submit. These signals are used to improve scoring, workflow design, and product usability.

Storage and tools

Feedback and product access requests may be collected using Microsoft Forms and may be reviewed or exported for internal analysis. Because our team, service providers, and technology tools may operate across different countries, submitted information may be processed or stored outside your country.

Sharing

We do not sell submitted respondent information. We may use service providers and software platforms to host the website, collect responses, store records, or operate the product.

Retention

We keep submitted information only as long as reasonably needed for product research, follow-up, and basic recordkeeping. You may ask us to delete your submitted information.

Your choices

You can choose not to submit forms or product feedback. You can also ask to correct or delete your submitted information by contacting us.

Contact

For privacy requests, data deletion requests, or questions about submitted information, contact: privacy@procuresignal.co.za

For general product questions, contact: hello@procuresignal.co.za

Important note

This is a practical early-stage privacy notice and should be reviewed by qualified legal counsel before a full expansion into paid subscriptions, large-scale customer onboarding, or expanded data processing.
