Disclaimer & Terms of Use

ProcureSignal is an independent procurement intelligence platform for procurement teams and growing businesses. By using this website, creating a product account, submitting feedback, or using the ProcureSignal workspace, you agree to the practical terms below.

Independent product

ProcureSignal is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or operated by any government entity, issuing authority, issuing buyer, or official tender portal.

Not an official tender source

ProcureSignal does not replace official tender portals, buyer websites, tender documents, bid documents, briefing notices, addenda, award notices, cancellation notices, or direct communication from issuing authorities.

No bid submission service

ProcureSignal does not submit bids, prepare bid packs, communicate with issuing authorities, or complete procurement submissions on behalf of users.

Verification required

Users must always verify official tender documents, addenda, deadlines, briefing requirements, eligibility rules, submission instructions, status changes, contact details, and official notices directly with the official issuing authority source before acting.

Product information

Information shown by ProcureSignal may be incomplete, delayed, duplicated, cancelled, amended, inaccurate, or based on limited public data. The product may classify some records as live opportunities, buyer-intelligence records, or not-actionable records for research and prioritization purposes only.

No tender-win prediction

Opportunity-fit scoring is a prioritization aid. It does not predict tender wins, guarantee eligibility, guarantee competitiveness, guarantee tender accuracy or availability, guarantee award outcomes, or guarantee that a bid should be submitted.

No professional advice

ProcureSignal does not provide legal, tax, financial, procurement, compliance, bid-submission, or professional advisory services. You should seek qualified professional advice for decisions that require it.

Use at your own risk

You are responsible for how you use information from ProcureSignal and for independently verifying all procurement opportunities before taking action.
